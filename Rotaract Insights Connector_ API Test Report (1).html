Defect

get_worldwide_rankings: district filter ignored

Request: type=district, district=3233, limit=1. Expected: District 3233. Received: District 1320, the first of 599 districts by member growth.

Scope: the district filter works with type=clubs, and the country filter works with type=district. Only the district filter combined with type=district fails.

Workaround: use get_districts or get_district_insights for single-district data.
